Pseudo-Labeling

Audit which labels a teacher saw before it labels anything.

A pseudo-label teacher must be trained without the validation labels it will help assess. One demonstration compares the common teacher shortcut with the corrected choice on three folds.

1. Which teacher never saw the validation labels

Which teacher never saw the validation labels

Which teacher excludes every validation label for the current fold?

Teacher selection is a dependency question before it is a confidence question. In ordinary K-fold training model k excludes fold k, and the other models include it. Dropping teacher k and averaging the rest therefore selects the teachers that saw the labels you are about to assess.

For validation fold 0, the shortcut teachers 1 and 2 between them saw 2 of the fold's 2 labeled rows, while teacher 0 saw 0. The difference is 2 - 0 = 2 validation labels that could leak into the pseudo-labels.

Worked steps

  1. Validation fold: 0; it holds 2 labeled rows.
  2. Shortcut selection excludes teacher 0 and averages teachers 1 and 2.
  3. Their training sets contain 2 of fold 0's labels.
  4. Corrected selection uses teacher 0, trained without fold 0: 0 labels.
  5. Leaked labels: 2 - 0 = 2.

Use the idea

For each outer validation fold, build the teacher from outer-training labels only, and keep its early stopping, calibration and later pseudo-label rounds inside that boundary.

Where the conclusion applies

Three folds with two labeled rows each, and teacher j trained on the other two folds. Confidence thresholds cannot repair a wrong teacher choice; this audit checks provenance, not pseudo-label quality.
