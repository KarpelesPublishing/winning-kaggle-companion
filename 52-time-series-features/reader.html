Chapter 52

Time Series Feature Engineering

Build lags from the history that exists at the forecast origin.

A lag helps only when it describes information available at the forecast origin. One demonstration shows which lags of 1, 2, 7 and 14 days survive as the horizon grows.

1. Which lags exist at a fixed forecast origin

Demonstration 1 of 1

Which lags exist at a fixed forecast origin

Which lagged target values are actually available when a multi-step forecast is made at a fixed origin?

A lag feature is usable only if the day it reads is at or before the forecast origin. A feature that is safe for a one-step forecast can read a future target at a longer horizon, so the feature builder must be told the origin. Short lags drop out first as the horizon grows, while long lags can still be missing because the history is short.

At horizon 3 the forecast day is 9 + 3 = 12, and lag 7 reads day 12 - 7 = 5. 1 of the 4 lags is observed, so 4 - 1 = 3 cannot be filled from history: lags 1 and 2 read days after the origin, so they must be dropped, replaced by predictions, or swapped for a longer lag; lag 14 reads before day 0 and stays missing.

Worked steps

  1. Horizon 3: forecast day 9 + 3 = 12.
  2. Lag 1: 12 - 1 = day 11, after the origin, so unavailable.
  3. Lag 2: 12 - 2 = day 10, after the origin, so unavailable.
  4. Lag 7: 12 - 7 = day 5, observed, value 19.
  5. Lag 14: 12 - 14 = day -2, before day 0, so missing.

Use the idea

Store the forecast origin with every example and build lags from the history as it stood at that origin. Test the builder by changing values published after the origin: its features must not move.

Where the conclusion applies

A constructed 10-day history, days 0 to 9, and one fixed origin. No new truth arrives between forecasts: a recursive forecast must supply predictions for the missing lags, and an online forecast that receives new observations is a different information contract. Lag 14 can point before day 0 and stays missing. No model is fitted.

Check your understanding: At which smallest horizon does lag 14 first read an observed day, and which day and value is that?
Horizon 5: the forecast day is 14, so lag 14 reads day 14 - 14 = 0, value 10. At horizon 4 it reads day -1, before the history begins.
