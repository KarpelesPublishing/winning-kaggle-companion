1. When a shaped reward prefers the policy that loses

Demonstration 1 of 1

When a shaped reward prefers the policy that loses

At what resource weight does an intermediate reward make a losing policy look better than a winning one?

Shaping adds a bonus for an intermediate event on top of the terminal reward. If the bonus is easy to earn without winning, a policy can collect it, lose the game, and still outscore a policy that wins. The terminal outcome stays the real objective, so a shaped return needs to be checked against win rate.

w is the resource reward weight, r_{i,t} the resource gained by policy i at step t (three steps), T_i its terminal reward (+5 for a win, -5 for a loss), and R_i the shaped return used to choose between policies.

Predict first. Policy 0 gathers 24 resource units but loses. Policy 1 gathers 3 and wins. At weight 0.5, which does the shaped return prefer?

At weight 0.5 the resource collector scores 0.5 x 24 + (-5) = 7.00 and the winner 0.5 x 3 + 5 = 6.50, so shaped return picks the losing policy by 7.00 - 6.50 = 0.50. The shaped objective now rewards a game it loses.

Worked steps

  1. Resource weight: w = 0.5.
  2. Resource collector: 0.5 x 24 + (-5) = 7.00, true outcome: loss.
  3. Winning policy: 0.5 x 3 + 5 = 6.50, true outcome: win.
  4. Shaped return prefers the loser.

Use the idea

Compare actual win rate with the shaped-return objective, and look in replays for resource-collection loops that lose games. Consider a properly specified potential-based shaping rule rather than a hand-weighted bonus.

Where the conclusion applies

Two constructed three-step policies with fixed returns, no discounting and no trained agent. Collecting resources is not assumed to cause winning. The crossover weight depends on these made-up numbers and shows an objective mismatch, not that all shaping fails.

Check your understanding: At what weight w do the two shaped returns become equal, and which policy leads just above it?
24w - 5 = 3w + 5 gives w = 10/21, about 0.48, where both score about 6.43. Above that weight the resource collector leads.
