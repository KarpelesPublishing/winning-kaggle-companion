Held-out seasons versus future prediction

When you hold out one season, how many of the training seasons come from after it?

Decide what a validation fold must imitate before choosing a splitter. Holding an entire season together protects its internal structure, but grouping alone does not enforce chronology. Compare the two training lists and ask which one matches the claim you want to make.

Validating on 2023: the all-other rule trains on 5 seasons, the earlier-only rule on 3, so 5 - 3 = 2 of the all-other seasons come after 2023. Those seasons could not be known when predicting 2023.

Worked steps

  1. Validation season: 2023.
  2. All-other training seasons: 5.
  3. Earlier-only training seasons: 3.
  4. Seasons after 2023 in the all-other list: 5 - 3 = 2.

Use the idea

Record prediction time, label availability and the entities that must stay together, then assert those boundaries in code before fitting anything.

Where the conclusion applies

Six ordered seasons, 2020 to 2025, and no fitted model. The count diagnoses chronology, not the size of any bias.

Check your understanding: With seasons 2020 to 2025 and 2024 held out, how many all-other training seasons come after it?
Only 2025, so one. The earlier-only list would train on 2020 to 2023.
