Playground Series Strategy

Screen experiments inside a budget, after the audit.

Use a bounded screening budget, protect time for validation, and choose experiments that resolve a named uncertainty. One demonstration picks the highest-value affordable set under an hour budget.

Spend a limited experiment budget after the audit

Which independent experiments fit a limited time budget once the validation audit has been paid for?

Reserve time for the fold and metric audit first, then choose bounded experiments that each resolve a named uncertainty. A budget can favor several short diagnostics over one long refinement, but only because of the values declared for each experiment.

Worked steps

  1. Budget: 6 hours, of which 2 go to the mandatory fold and metric audit.
  2. Experiments (assumed hours, value): Representation diagnostics 2 h, 4; External-data provenance check 3 h, 5; Long hyperparameter refinement 5 h, 6.
  3. 3 of the 8 combinations fit within 6 hours.
  4. Highest value: External-data provenance check with 5, using 2 + 3 = 5 hours.
  5. Unused: 6 - 5 = 1 hour.

Use the idea

Estimate each experiment's runtime and the decision it informs before scheduling it. Treat the schedule as a budget to spend, not a checklist to exhaust, and freeze a runnable submission early.

Where the conclusion applies

Costs and learning values are illustrative planning assumptions, not measured score gains. The audit is mandatory here, and experiments are independent and indivisible. The chosen set is only the highest assumed value; change the values for your own project.
