Counting subjects that cross the validation boundary

How many subjects cross your train and validation boundary under each split?

A sensor row is a window, but the person producing it may be the real unit of generalization. The control switches between an interleaved row split and a subject holdout, and the activity counts how many identities appear on both sides. No classifier is trained: the point is to make the evaluation boundary visible.

S_train is the set of subject identities among training windows, S_val the set among validation windows, and n_shared the number of identities in both.

Predict first. Four subjects contribute three windows each, stored subject by subject (windows 0 to 2 are subject 0, 3 to 5 subject 1, and so on). The interleaved split sends every third window, 0, 3, 6 and 9, to validation. How many subjects appear on both sides?

The subject holdout puts 9 + 3 = 12 windows on the two sides. Validation holds 1 subject, of which 0 also appear in training, so 1 - 0 = 1 validation subject is unseen. No person is on both sides, though that alone does not show a matching activity mix or leaderboard agreement.

Worked steps

  1. Split: subject holdout.
  2. Windows: 9 training + 3 validation = 12.
  3. Subjects in validation: 1.
  4. Of those, also in training: 0.
  5. Validation subjects the model has never seen: 1 - 0 = 1.

Use the idea

Define the real test holdout, split subjects before window construction, and assert that no identity or overlapping window is reused. Keep the failing random-split result as diagnostic evidence, not a model-selection score.

Where the conclusion applies

Twelve constructed windows from four subjects, stored subject by subject; the subject holdout sends the last subject's three windows to validation. Window leakage and subject leakage are different checks. Zero shared subjects establishes identity separation only; it cannot establish matching activity prevalence, that a held-out person has every class, or an accurate leaderboard estimate.

Check your understanding: After the subject split reports 0 shared subjects, what has been established and what has not?
Only that no person appears on both sides: 1 validation subject, 0 of them also in training. It does not show that the held-out person performs every activity or that the validation score will match the leaderboard.
