Chapter 21

Financial Competitions: Time, Regime, and Leakage

Purge training labels whose forward interval reaches validation.

Time-ordered financial data needs boundaries built from label availability, not row counts. One demonstration shows how a longer forward label horizon purges more training rows before validation.

1. Purging labels that reach into validation

Demonstration 1 of 1

Purging labels that reach into validation

Which training rows must you purge when their forward-looking labels extend into the validation period?

A correctly built feature that looks backward can use past history at the boundary, but a training label that looks forward can reveal outcomes the model would not know at its fitting cutoff. Under the closed-interval rule, any training row whose label ends at or after the validation start is purged. A longer horizon removes more rows.

n_purged is the number of purged training rows, T the set of training times 0 to 7, t one training time, h the forward label horizon in time steps, and v the validation start (8). A row's label interval is closed and runs from t to t + h.

With horizon 3, the last time kept is 4 + 3 = 7, still before 8. The next time, 5 + 3 = 8, reaches the validation start, so it and any later times are purged: 3 purged and 5 kept.

Worked steps

  1. Label horizon h = 3; validation starts at 8.
  2. A row at time t is kept only if t + 3 < 8, that is t < 5.
  3. Last kept time: 4 + 3 = 7.
  4. First purged time: 5 + 3 = 8, which touches 8.
  5. Purged times: 5, 6, 7 (3 of 8).

Use the idea

Write prediction time, feature availability and label end time as separate fields, then purge by elapsed time rather than a guessed row gap. Split by distinct timestamps so assets sharing a time stay on one side.

Where the conclusion applies

Eight integer training timestamps, validation starting at 8, and closed label intervals. The purge follows the label horizon, not the length of a feature's backward lookback. Purging removes boundary rows but cannot reveal a target-derived predictor already present in the validation features.

Check your understanding: With a label horizon of 5, how many of the eight training times are purged?
Five, times 3 to 7: time 3 ends at 3 + 5 = 8, which touches the validation start, while time 2 ends at 2 + 5 = 7 and is kept.
