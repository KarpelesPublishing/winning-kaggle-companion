Apply this to your model

Inspect sample rate, recording IDs, species support, secondary labels, and the official scoring time window. Split recordings or broader required groups before creating training windows, then report class support in each fold without promising impossible stratification. Match noise lengths and measure actual mixed power before clipping or normalization. Tune spectrogram windows in physical milliseconds and compare augmentation on held-out recordings. Define trusted negatives explicitly in weak-label masks. Aggregate and calibrate on the exact scored unit. Reject gains from recording overlap, positive-only supervision collapse, or thresholds fitted to a different aggregation distribution.
