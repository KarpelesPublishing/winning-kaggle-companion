Choosing 'audit entities and future holdout' commits you to collecting IDs, timestamps, label availability first, then to: build isolated chronological split. 4 - 1 = 3 other actions wait until that evidence exists. The choice predicts no score gain; it makes the first test explicit.

Worked steps

  1. Selected action: audit entities and future holdout.
  2. Evidence required: IDs, timestamps, label availability.
  3. Next step: build isolated chronological split.
  4. Actions left for later: 4 - 1 = 3.

Use the idea

Record one experiment for your competition: what information it adds, how you will test it, and what observation would make you abandon it.

Where the conclusion applies

The four actions are indexed choices, not ranked by expected performance. No gain is claimed for any of them.

Check your understanding: Why does tuning come after auditing the holdout?
Tuning optimizes a validation score; if the folds leak future or entity information, that score rewards the leak.
