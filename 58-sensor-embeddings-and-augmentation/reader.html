What the result means

A double reflection should recover each selected triple and ID, but partial reflection can create duplicate anatomical assignments. A mechanically reversible transform does not prove a physiologically valid sample; pair completeness, axis alignment, and label invariance still need verification.

Apply this to your model

Inspect the sensor coordinate frame and channel order before applying anatomical symmetry. Choose a single explicit sensor-by-axis input contract and propagate it through the encoder, location IDs, augmentation, and inference. Reflect only label-preserving classes, negating the verified lateral component and remapping left/right identities together. Assert shape compatibility and double-reflection recovery; inspect paired sensors for valid placement. Compare location embeddings, training reflection, and TTA as separate ablations on subject-held-out folds. Reject the transform when axis conventions are unverified, asymmetric labels change meaning, or gains come from an inconsistent baseline ledger.
