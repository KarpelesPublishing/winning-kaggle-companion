Sensor Embeddings and Augmentation

Write down sensor and axis meaning before you reflect anything.

A reflection that undoes itself can still be invalid

Does a sensor reflection keep a coherent ID and location contract, and does it undo itself when applied twice?

A reflection that negates x and swaps left and right IDs is its own inverse, so applying it twice returns the original values exactly. That exact recovery says nothing about whether the in-between sample is valid: reflecting only part of a sensor pair gives two sensors the same anatomical ID.

Reflecting the first 4 sensors leaves 4 distinct IDs among 4 sensors, so 4 - 4 = 0 duplicates. Every left and right partner was reflected together, so the IDs stay unique.

Worked steps

  1. Reflect the first 4 sensors: negate x and swap left and right IDs.
  2. IDs after one reflection: 2 1 4 3.
  3. Distinct IDs: 4, so duplicates are 4 - 4 = 0.
  4. Reflect again: recovered exactly = yes.

Use the idea

Assert shape compatibility and double-reflection recovery, then also check that paired sensors are reflected together. Confirm which axis is truly lateral, and reflect only labels that keep their meaning under symmetry.

Where the conclusion applies

Four sensors with made-up coordinates and an assumed anatomical frame in which x is lateral. Partial reflection is shown as a diagnostic, not a recommended augmentation. Recovering the data does not show that activity labels stay valid under reflection.

Check your understanding: Reflect the first 3 sensors. Which two sensors share an ID after one reflection, and are the data recovered after two?
Sensors 2 and 3 both hold ID 4 (IDs 2 1 4 4, so 4 - 3 = 1 duplicate), because sensor 3 was not reflected. Two reflections still recover the data exactly.
