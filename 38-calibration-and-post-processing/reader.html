Calibration and Post-Processing

Probability magnitude matters even when ranking remains unchanged. Apply this to your model

Inspect OOF probability alignment, reliability-bin counts and the competition metric. Separate base fitting, calibration fitting and final evaluation labels. Test a smooth sigmoid or positive-temperature mapping first; consider isotonic only with sufficient calibration support and awareness of ties. Fit each model’s mapping on its own corresponding scores, or calibrate the completed blend as a distinct pipeline candidate. Compare log loss/Brier score, reliability and ranking on untouched rows. Verify any inverse target transform against a fitted mapping. Reject calibration when gains disappear out of sample, bins are too sparse or a batch-rank operation is mistaken for an inverse transform.
