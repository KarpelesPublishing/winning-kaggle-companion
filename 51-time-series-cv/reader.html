Time Series Cross-Validation

Keep only training labels that exist at the forecast origin.

Define the forecast origin, the horizon and when each label becomes available, then remove training rows that would peek ahead. One demonstration shows how many labels a row gap leaves unavailable.

Which training labels are still unknown at the forecast origin

Which training labels are still unavailable at a forecast origin after choosing a row gap?

The validation origin is day 20 and each row's label covers the next five days. Excluding rows just before the origin removes training rows whose labels would arrive late. A gap is justified by label availability, not by autocorrelation alone.

t is a training day, t_0 the validation origin (day 20), g the number of excluded rows just before the origin, and h the forward-label horizon (5 days), so a row's label ends on day t + h.

A row on day t has its label at day t + 5, which must be before day 20, so any day up to 14 is safe. With a gap of 5 the last retained day is 14: 14 - 14 = 0 retained rows have labels not yet available at the origin. The gap equals or exceeds the horizon, so none overlap.

Worked steps

  1. Origin day 20, horizon 5 days, gap 5 rows.
  2. Retained training days: 0 to 14, which is 15 rows.
  3. A label is available when day + 5 < 20, so days up to 14 are safe; the latest safe retained day is 14.
  4. Unavailable labels: 14 - 14 = 0.
  5. Rows removed beyond the strictly necessary: 0.

Use the idea

Write down the origin, horizon, label end time and reporting delay for each row, then assert label availability in every fold. Fit scalers and other preprocessing only within the retained training rows.

Where the conclusion applies

One row per day, labels must end strictly before day 20, and this is a forward split, not a general purged K-fold. A different data latency or interval convention would change the gap that is enough.

Check your understanding: A gap of 10 also gives 0 unavailable labels. What does it cost compared with a gap of 5?
It keeps 10 training rows instead of 15, so 15 - 10 = 5 rows from days 10 to 14, whose labels are already available, are dropped for no label-availability reason.
