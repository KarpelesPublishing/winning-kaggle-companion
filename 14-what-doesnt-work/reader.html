1. Does the pseudo-label teacher depend on held-out labels?

Demonstration 1 of 1

Does the pseudo-label teacher depend on held-out labels?

Did the teacher that produced the pseudo-labels learn from the labels of the rows you are validating on?

Pseudo-labels are predictions used as temporary training labels, so their safety depends on how the teacher was trained. Sharing a pseudo-label set is not contamination by itself; sharing a teacher that consumed the held-out labels is.

The teacher used 4 labels, 4 from training rows and 0 from validation rows 4 and 5: 4 - 4 = 0. The count is zero, so the two pseudo-rows do not depend on held-out labels. That does not show the pseudo-labels are accurate or useful.

Worked steps

  1. Teacher training rows: 0, 1, 2, 3.
  2. Validation rows: 4, 5.
  3. Overlap between the two: 0 rows.
  4. Safe flag: 1 (no held-out dependency).

Use the idea

For each outer fold, fit the teacher on that fold's training slice only, add pseudo-labels to student training only, and assess the student on genuine held-out labels against a no-pseudo baseline.

Where the conclusion applies

Eight rows, two validation rows and two illustrative pseudo-rows with no fitted model. A zero count is necessary for this boundary, not evidence that the pseudo-labels are accurate or help the student.
