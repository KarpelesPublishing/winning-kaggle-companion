Is this note ready to reuse as a conditional rule?

Does a competition note record the condition, evidence, failure case and next test that make it reusable?

A reusable note should say when to act, what observation supports the action, when it may fail and what to test next. The worksheet checks three constructed validation notes for those four fields. It checks completeness, not whether the technique is true.

Use the idea

Pick one note from your own notebook, find its weakest field and rewrite it. Add the case where your advice would change and the observation that would make you revise the rule in the next competition.

Where the conclusion applies

Three constructed notes with different completeness. The worksheet checks evidence fields, not whether a technique is true. Only evidence from the next task can test whether a principle transfers, and a useful counterexample narrows the rule without making it useless.
