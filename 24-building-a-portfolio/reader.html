The modeling goal requires 3 artifacts and the example portfolio holds 2, so 3 - 2 = 1 is missing: ablation report. A gap is a prompt to produce evidence, not a prediction of hiring success.

Worked steps

  1. Goal: Modeling.
  2. Required artifacts: 1 (executable baseline), 2 (validation rationale), 3 (ablation report).
  3. Present in the example portfolio: 2 of 3.
  4. Missing: 3 - 2 = 1.

Use the idea

Pick one missing artifact you can finish from an existing competition and write a one-sentence acceptance criterion, such as another analyst rerunning one ablation and explaining its validation choice.

Where the conclusion applies

A decision worksheet over a constructed portfolio, not an employment-value score. The three goals encode three reader goals, and the artifact list is illustrative. A missing artifact predicts nothing about hiring or placement, and a platform tier does not certify the missing properties.

Check your understanding: For the domain analysis goal, which required artifact is missing?
Error-segment analysis (artifact 5). Domain analysis needs artifacts 2, 4 and 5; the portfolio holds 2 and 4, so 3 - 2 = 1 is missing.
