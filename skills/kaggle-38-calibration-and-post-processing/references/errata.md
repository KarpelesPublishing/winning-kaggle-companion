# Technical corrections

These corrections describe the supplied October 7, 2026 baseline and are incorporated into the revised local manuscript. Historical competition scores remain the author's reports; companion activities are constructed teaching cases. The revision records are source-bound, and a rebuilt artifact does not independently reproduce historical scores.

| Book location | Correction | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Chapters 10 and 29, interaction/feature acceptance | Minimize RMSE and log loss; maximize AUC and accuracy. A larger RMSE is worse. | [Scikit-learn scoring conventions](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/model_evaluation.html); `tests/test_modeling.py` tests both directions. |
| Chapter 20, micro-F1 | For exhaustive, single-label multiclass prediction, micro-F1 equals accuracy. Class-specific thresholds may change predicted labels, but both metrics then change together. This equality does not cover multilabel tasks or scoring only a subset of classes. | [Scikit-learn classification metrics](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/model_evaluation.html#multiclass-and-multilabel-classification). |
| Chapter 34; Chapter 63 pseudo-labeling; Appendix B, Mistake 9 | For outer fold k, select teachers whose training rows exclude fold k. In an ordinary K-fold teacher collection that means teacher k, not all teachers except k. For multiple teachers, fit them only within the outer training rows. Keep the original validation rows separate and score only genuine held-out labels. | Direct training-index trace in `tests/test_checks.py`; [leakage and pipelines](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/common_pitfalls.html#data-leakage). |
| Chapters 9, 26 and 36, target-derived features | Outer-validation labels must not enter preprocessing of outer-training rows. Inner cross-fitting belongs inside each outer training fold. Fit a validation mapping and unseen-category prior only on that outer training fold. | [Target encoder cross-fitting](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/auto_examples/preprocessing/plot_target_encoder_cross_val.html). |
| Chapters 38 and 47, ECE | Standard ECE weights each bin's absolute calibration error by the fraction of observations in that bin. The unweighted mean of errors from occupied bins is a different statistic. | [Guo et al., 2017](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/guo17a.html), equation 3; independent 90/10-bin counterexample gives 0.14 weighted versus 0.50 unweighted. |
| Chapters 38 and 47, calibration | Fit calibration on data independent of base-model fitting, and evaluate the fitted calibrator on data independent of calibration fitting. Isotonic calibration with an increasing constraint is monotone; it does not fit a non-monotone curve. | [Scikit-learn probability calibration](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/calibration.html). |
| Chapter 59, softmax | Subtract each row's maximum logit before exponentiating. The raw exponentiation of logits near 1000 overflows. | [SciPy softmax](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.special.softmax.html); `tests/test_checks.py`. |
| Chapters 15, 54 and 55; Appendix B; index, Santa 2024 | Santa 2024 was The Perplexity Permutation Puzzle. Pancake sorting may be taught as a separate constructed optimization example, but it should not be identified as that competition. | [Official Kaggle competition](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/santa-2024/overview/prizes). |
| Chapters 3, 6 and 51, temporal CV | Holding out one year while training on every other year is not strict future prediction when later years are included. Forward validation trains only on information available before the validation period. Group isolation and chronology answer different questions. | [TimeSeriesSplit](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.model_selection.TimeSeriesSplit.html). |

Additional candidate corrections are documented in the private editorial review. They are not silently presented here as verified competition history. Small companion experiments illustrate a method's assumptions; they do not establish that a particular method wins competitions or guarantees a metric gain.
