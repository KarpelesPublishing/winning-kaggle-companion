The Five-Stage Feature Engineering Process

Compare raw and log target functionals.

A staged process keeps a feature set explainable, and a target transform changes the loss a model optimizes. One demonstration scores the arithmetic mean and the back-transformed log mean on raw squared error.

1. Raw and log target functionals

Demonstration 1 of 1

Raw and log target functionals

When the target has a heavy upper tail, how far apart are the arithmetic mean and the back-transformed log mean, and what does that cost on raw squared error?

Squared error on raw values and squared error on log values optimize different summaries of the same distribution. Among constant predictors the arithmetic mean minimizes raw squared error. The log summary is less pulled by the large observation, which can suit another loss while hurting raw MSE.

With the tail at 200, the arithmetic mean is 76.67 and the log-mean prediction is 34.94. Scored on raw squared error the log summary costs 9363 - 7622 = 1741 more, because among constant predictors the mean minimizes raw MSE. Log fitting optimizes another functional.

Worked steps

  1. Tail observation: 200.
  2. Arithmetic mean prediction: 76.67.
  3. Log-mean prediction after expm1: 34.94.
  4. Raw MSE of the mean: 7622; of the log-mean prediction: 9363.
  5. Extra raw MSE from the log summary: 9363 - 7622 = 1741.

Use the idea

Write the competition metric beside your target-transform hypothesis, then compare inverted predictions on fixed held-out rows under the actual score. Do not choose a transform from a cleaner histogram alone.

Where the conclusion applies

Three positive targets and constant predictors, which isolate the loss functional from any model. A fitted model, other metrics and retransformation corrections are outside this example, so it shows direction, not the size of a real effect.

Check your understanding: At a tail value of 200, how much raw MSE does the log-mean prediction add compared with the arithmetic mean?
About 1741: the log-mean prediction scores 9363 and the arithmetic mean 7622 on raw MSE, so 9363 - 7622 = 1741. At a tail of 20 the same gap is only about 0.55.
